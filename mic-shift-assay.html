MIC Shift Assay Builder

Design a broth microdilution MIC shift panel from two built-in control groups plus any additional experimental drugs/species, then test how physiologic human and mouse serum albumin and serum shift each MIC. You can also switch to serum preincubation stability mode with custom incubation times (with or without albumin), set hypotheses, watch figures update in real time, and export everything as a FAIR data package.

1 · Assay design — drugs × species

Two control panels of two drugs against three species each (2-for-3, 2-for-3). You can also add extra experimental drugs and species; every added drug is crossed against every added species.

Panel summary

12 combinations

Each of the 4 drugs is tested against its group's 3 species, always as a broth microdilution doubling-dilution series, giving 12 baseline drug–species MICs before any serum or albumin is added.

Defaults pair two low/high protein-binding Gram-negative agents with Gram-negatives, and two low/high protein-binding Gram-positive agents with Gram-positives — a spread deliberately chosen to illustrate that protein binding, not spectrum, drives the size of the shift.

2 · Select assay criteria — serum & albumin conditions

Choose direct additive MIC-shift mode or serum preincubation stability mode. Concentrations and setup settings are editable per run.

Preincubation settings

serum stability mode

The control MIC (cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton broth, no additive) is always recorded and is the denominator for every fold-shift below. Physiologic human serum albumin runs ~35–50 g/L; mouse serum albumin is somewhat lower, commonly cited near 25–35 g/L. Whole-serum supplementation is conventionally run at 50% v/v as a readable compromise, or up to 90–100% v/v to approximate fully bound in vivo conditions — edit the percentage to match your protocol.

3 · Record broth microdilution MIC results

One row per drug–species combination. Enter known control MIC (optional) and measured MIC in mg/L (equivalent to µg/mL); leave a cell blank if that condition was not assayed.

4 · Real-time fold-shift figures

MIC of each additive condition divided by the control MIC, plotted as log₂ fold. Bars above the dashed line at +2 represent a ≥4-fold rise (reduced apparent potency); below −2 is a ≥4-fold fall.

Why ±4-fold? Broth microdilution reads out on a doubling-dilution series, so a single well of assay noise is already a 2-fold difference. A shift has to clear two doubling dilutions (4-fold) before it is distinguishable from ordinary well-to-well variability — that is the conventional bar for calling a real MIC shift rather than noise.

5 · Shift verdicts

Flags every recorded condition that clears the ≥4-fold bar.

9 · Bench procedure

    

      
    
        
  1. Prepare doubling dilutions of each drug in cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton broth (CAMHB), conventionally spanning at least 0.03–64 mg/L, adjusted to bracket the expected MIC.
    2. 
        
  2. Prepare additive broths in parallel: CAMHB + human serum albumin at your chosen g/L, CAMHB + mouse serum albumin, CAMHB + human serum at your chosen % v/v, and CAMHB + mouse serum, each carried through the same doubling-dilution series as the control.
    3. 
        
  3. Standardize the inoculum to ~5 × 105 CFU/mL in each well at the point of inoculation (typically a 1:100 dilution of a 0.5 McFarland suspension), identically across control and additive panels.
    4. 
        
  4. Incubate 16–20 h at 35°C in ambient air (extend or adjust per organism, e.g. 20–24 h for some fastidious species), then read the lowest concentration with no visible growth as the MIC.
    5. 
        
  5. Read serum/albumin wells carefully. Serum and concentrated albumin add background turbidity and can obscure the growth/no-growth endpoint — read against a matched uninoculated additive-only blank at the same concentration, and consider a colorimetric viability indicator if turbidity alone is ambiguous.
    6. 
        
  6. Include growth and sterility controls on every plate: inoculated drug-free broth (growth control) and uninoculated highest-concentration broth (sterility control), for the base medium and for each additive condition.
    7. 
        
  7. Enter every MIC as you read it in section 3 — the fold-shift figures and verdicts in sections 4–5 recompute immediately, so an implausible shift is visible before the plates are discarded.
