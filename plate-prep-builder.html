Plate Prep Builder

Design a 96- or 384-well dilution series — serial log by addition (spiking stock into every well, no carryover) or half-log by transfer (classic serial carryover dilution) — as a simple 2D plate (one sample per plate) or a 3D/blocked plate (several samples per plate, including a 6-block 384-well layout). Pick pipettes from a fixed 16-channel and 12-channel P100 plus an adjustable-spacing P20 (Vmax 300 µL, also usable with 8-tube strips), watch the plate and prep steps update live, enter sample labels and recorded values per well, and export everything as a FAIR data package.

1 · Plate format & layout

Choose the plate, whether it is one sample (2D) or several blocked samples (3D), and which axis the dilution series runs along.

Pipette fleet

editable min/max volume ranges, µL

2 · Dilution series design

Pick a mode and factor, or start from a preset.

Enter a molecular weight, then switch the concentration unit above to automatically convert the top and stock concentrations between µg/mL and µM.

3 · Working stock preparation

How much of the starting (source) stock solution and diluent to combine to make the working stock used by the dilution series above.

The working stock is the addition-mode spiking stock (section 2 "stock concentration") or, in transfer mode, the premixed well-1 top-concentration stock. Each block below can override its own source stock and target concentrations/factor; otherwise it inherits these defaults.

4 · Live plate

Wells are shaded by concentration (log scale); click a well for its volumes and to enter a recorded value.

High conc.Low conc.Gray = blank

Click any well to see its step, volumes, recommended pipette, and to record a value.

5 · Blocks / samples

One row per block (= one sample). A 2D plate always has a single block. Tick "Custom" to give a block its own dilution factor, top/stock concentration, unit, molecular weight, and source stock — e.g. factors X, Y, Z run side by side on the same plate.

6 · Generated prep steps

One row per dilution-series step; repeat across every block and replicate listed.

7 · Well-by-well data entry

Enter a recorded value and notes per well. Feeds the FAIR export's sample_values.csv.

8 · Dataset metadata & FAIR export

Everything above, plus this metadata, is packaged as a Frictionless tabular data package.
